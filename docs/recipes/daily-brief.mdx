Goal: start each day knowing what needs you, and end it knowing what got done.

**You need**

- To be the owner or an admin to change the times. Everyone can look.
- Google Calendar connected, if you want today's meetings in the brief.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the schedule">
    Open your account menu, then **Settings**, then **Schedule**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your times">
    Set **Morning brief** (7am to 11am) and **End-of-day report** (5pm to 9pm, or **Off**). Emplio's working day runs from the brief to an hour after the report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include your staff">
    Leave **Staff briefs and reports** on so each person gets their own, about their own meetings, tasks and emails. It stays silent when there's nothing for them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. Keep **Background rounds** on anything but **Off**, or there are no briefs or reports.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell it what to watch">
    ```text
    Add to your always-check list: new enquiries in Gmail and invoices more than 30 days overdue.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** a short brief in your main chat each morning, and on Telegram if you've connected it. Up to five points: today's meetings, what's late, what's due and what needs your decision. In the evening, a report of what was done, not done, couldn't be done and what's next.

<Tip>Reply to the brief right there. Your answer carries on from it, like any chat.</Tip>
