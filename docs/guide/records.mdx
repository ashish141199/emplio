Emplio keeps structured records, like leads, orders or stock, in its own database, and reads and updates them as it works. Owners and admins can view them under **Memory**, then **Database**.

## Make or change a table

Ask in a chat: "Keep a table of our leads with name, phone, source and status." Emplio makes it and fills it in. To add a column or fix rows, just ask again.

## View a table

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a table">
    Choose it from the list on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search, sort and filter">
    Use **Search this table**. Click a column's name to sort by it, or its filter button to filter it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Press **Export to Excel** to download what you see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page is read-only. Changes go through Emplio.

## Who can use it

| | Owners and admins | Staff |
|---|---|---|
| See the **Database** tab | Yes | No |
| Use tables through Emplio | All tables | All except **Private** ones |
| Make or change tables | Yes | No |

Emplio marks tables private when they hold things staff shouldn't see, like pay or bank details. Turn **Private** on or off at the top of a table.
