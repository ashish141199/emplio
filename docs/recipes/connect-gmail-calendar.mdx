Goal: Emplio can work in your email and calendar, and asks you before it sends or changes anything.

**You need**

- A Google account. Each person connects their own, so Emplio works with your inbox when you ask and theirs when they ask.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/app/integrations-light.png" alt="The Integrations window listing apps to connect, with a search box" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/app/integrations-dark.png" alt="The Integrations window listing apps to connect, with a search box" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Open your account menu, then **Integrations**, then **Browse**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Gmail">
    Search for **Gmail** and click **Connect**. Sign in with Google in the window that opens. It closes by itself when you're done.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Google Calendar">
    Do the same for **Google Calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the connections">
    Open **My connections**. Each app should say **Connected**. If one says it expired, click **Reconnect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    ```text
    What's on my calendar tomorrow, and which emails from today still need a reply?
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** Emplio can read, search, draft and send email, and see, create and move events. Looking things up never asks. Sending, changing or deleting shows a card: **Allow**, **Always allow** or **Deny**.

<Tip>To stop the cards for one action, tap **Always allow**. To undo it, go to **Settings**, then **Agent**, and remove it under **Always allowed**. The owner or an admin can switch **Before sending or changing things** to **Just do it** for the whole business.</Tip>
