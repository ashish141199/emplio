Emplio is the same product either way. Emplio Cloud is run for you. Self-hosted is free and runs on your own computer or server.

| | Self-hosted | Emplio Cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Free, plus your own AI and other provider bills | Paid plans |
| Computer | A container on your machine | Managed for you |
| Browser | Plain Chromium; some sites will block it | Managed, with protection against blocking |
| Links to apps Emplio starts | Not available yet | Yes |
| Keys and setup | Yours | Done for you |

## Pick Emplio Cloud if

- You want to start in minutes, with nothing to install.
- Emplio needs to use websites that block ordinary browsers.
- You want links to apps Emplio builds and starts on its computer.

## Pick self-hosted if

- You want everything on your own machine.
- You're happy to set up and pay for your own AI and app keys.
- You want to change the code. It's open source under AGPL-3.0.

<Note>Self-hosted Emplio has no billing and is never paused. You pay your AI and other providers directly.</Note>
