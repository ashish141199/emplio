Goal: Emplio follows your way of doing things without being told twice.

**You need**

- Nothing set up. Any chat works, on the web or on Telegram.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say the rule">
    ```text
    From now on, give new cafés 30 days' credit.
    ```
    Emplio saves it as a note in company memory and uses it from then on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it in Memory">
    Click **Memory** in the sidebar, then **Notes**. Company rules are under **Company**. Search by any word in the note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it">
    Open the note and click **Suggest a change**. A chat opens about that note. Say what's different:

    ```text
    Make it 15 days for cafés outside the city.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Undo a change (optional)">
    Every note keeps its full history, so nothing is lost.

    ```text
    Put the credit note back the way it was yesterday.
    ```
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** plain notes you can read, kept for the whole team.

| Folder | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Everyone in the business |
| **Restricted** | The owner and admins. For money, pay, HR and owner notes. |
| **Mine** | Only you |

<Note>When staff change a company note, it becomes a proposal. The owner sees it in the morning brief.</Note>
