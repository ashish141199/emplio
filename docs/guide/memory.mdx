Emplio writes down what it learns as plain notes: your clients, prices, rules and how things are done. Open **Memory** in the sidebar, then **Notes**, to read them.

## Who sees what

| Folder | Who can see it |
|---|---|
| **Company** | Everyone in the business. |
| **Restricted** | Owners and admins only. |
| **Mine** | Only you. |

Every change is kept with full history, so nothing is lost.

## Teach it

Just tell Emplio, in any chat: "Give new cafés 30 days' credit". It saves the rule and uses it from then on.

## Change a note

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the note">
    Pick it from the list, or use **Search notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Suggest a change">
    A new chat opens with the note already named. Say what to change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Notes are read-only here so every change goes through Emplio. When staff change a company note, it goes to the owner as a proposal.

<Tip>**Memory** also holds [Files](/guide/files) and, for owners and admins, the [Database](/guide/records).</Tip>
