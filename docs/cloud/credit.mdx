Your usage is shown as a share of the month, not as money. When it reaches 100%, Emplio pauses until you top up or the month renews.

## The meter

The usage meter in the sidebar shows how much of this month's usage you've used, as a percentage.

| At | What happens |
|---|---|
| 80% | The owner and admins get an email. |
| 100% | The owner and admins get an email, and Emplio pauses. |

## While Emplio is paused

- A **paused** banner shows on the web.
- Emplio doesn't reply on the web or on any channel, Telegram included.
- Automations don't run. Each one shows a run that didn't happen, and why.
- Emplio's own tasks wait, then start once there's usage again.

## Get it working again

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & usage">
    The owner or an admin opens the account menu and clicks **Billing & usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up or upgrade">
    Buy a top-up, or move to a bigger plan. An upgrade takes effect right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Or wait: usage starts again when your month renews.

<Tip>Running out every month? An upgrade is usually simpler than topping up. Growth gives 2.5 times Starter's usage and Pro 5 times.</Tip>
