Everyone who takes part in Emplio is expected to treat others well, in issues, pull requests and anywhere else the project meets.

## In short

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- No harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Read the project's [code of conduct](https://github.com/emplio-app/emplio/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) on GitHub.

<Note>Report anything that breaks it to **ashish@kulp.ai**. We'll keep it confidential and act on it.</Note>
