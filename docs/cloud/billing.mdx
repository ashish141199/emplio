The owner and admins manage the plan from **Billing & usage**. Nobody else on the team sees it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing & usage">
    Open your account menu and click **Billing & usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick or change your plan">
    Choose a plan, the 7-day pass, or a top-up. See [Plans and usage](/cloud/plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Your billing country decides how you pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How you pay

| Billing country | Paid through |
|---|---|
| India | Razorpay |
| Everywhere else | Stripe |

<Warning>The payment method is set by your billing country and can't be switched later.</Warning>

## Changing your plan

| Change | When it happens |
|---|---|
| Upgrade | Right away. You pay only the difference for the rest of the month. |
| Downgrade | At your next renewal |
| Cancel | At your next renewal |

Prices are on the [pricing page](https://emplio.app/pricing).
