Goal: overdue invoices get followed up every week without you remembering to do it.

**You need**

- Zoho Books or Zoho Invoice connected, or your invoices kept in Emplio's [records](/guide/records).
- Gmail connected, to send the reminders.
- To be the owner or an admin. Only they can set up automations.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your accounts app">
    Open your account menu, then **Integrations**, then **Browse**. Find **Zoho Books** or **Zoho Invoice** and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask once, by hand">
    ```text
    Which invoices are unpaid and more than 30 days overdue? List customer, amount and days late.
    ```
    Check the list looks right before you automate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give the job its own agent">
    ```text
    Set up an Invoice Chaser agent for payment follow-ups.
    ```
    The agent appears on the **Team** page under **Agents**. It keeps lessons from each job it does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put it on a schedule">
    ```text
    Every Monday at 10am, have the Invoice Chaser check overdue invoices and email each customer a polite reminder.
    ```
    Emplio saves this as an automation and shows a card asking you to allow what it will send on every run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check what it may do">
    Open your account menu, then **Automations**. Each automation lists the actions it sends or changes, with a switch for each. Anything not switched on is refused when it runs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** a run every Monday in its own chat, and a line or two in your main chat on what came of it. Routine runs with nothing for you stay quiet.

<Tip>Tell it your exceptions once: "Never chase Mehta Stores, they pay every quarter." Emplio keeps the rule in company memory.</Tip>
