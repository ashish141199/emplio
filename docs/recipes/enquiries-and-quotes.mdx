Goal: every enquiry in your inbox gets saved as a lead and answered, with a proper quote when the customer asks for one.

**You need**

- Gmail connected. See [Connect Gmail and Calendar](/recipes/connect-gmail-calendar).
- Your price list, as a file or a message.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give it your prices once">
    Attach your price list to a message and say:

    ```text
    This is our price list. Use it for every quote.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it to go through the enquiries">
    ```text
    Go through this week's enquiries in Gmail, add them to our leads, and draft replies.
    ```
    Emplio shows each step as it works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the drafts">
    Before it saves or sends anything in Gmail, a card asks you. Tap **Allow**, **Always allow** or **Deny**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a quote">
    ```text
    Make a quote PDF for Sharma Traders: 200 kg basmati at our wholesale rate, 30 days' credit.
    ```
    The PDF appears in the chat. Open it to download it or get a share link. It is also kept in **Memory** under **Files**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make it automatic (optional)">
    ```text
    Whenever a new enquiry email comes in, add it to Leads and draft a reply.
    ```
    Emplio saves this as an [automation](/guide/automations). Only the owner and admins can set one up.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** a Leads table that stays up to date, replies waiting for your OK, and quotes made from your own prices.

<Tip>Correct a draft once ("we never offer free delivery under 50 kg") and Emplio keeps that rule for next time. See [Teach it a rule](/recipes/teach-a-rule).</Tip>
