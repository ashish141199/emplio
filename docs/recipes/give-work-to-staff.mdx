Goal: hand out work once and let Emplio do the chasing.

**You need**

- Your team added on the **Team** page.
- Telegram connected for each person, if you want reminders on their phones. See [Channels](/guide/channels).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set who reports to whom">
    On the **Team** page, open a person's menu and choose **Set who they report to**. Managers then see and change their team's tasks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a task">
    On **Work**, type in the task box, pick the person under **Who**, and set a due date. Or ask in the chat:

    ```text
    Ask Rahul to send Acme the revised quote by Friday 5pm.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let Emplio chase">
    When a task comes due, the person gets one reminder in their chat and on Telegram, during their working hours (9am to 8pm their time). After that, their morning brief lists it as late.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hear about late work">
    Two days late, Emplio tells the person they report to. With no manager set, it tells whoever asked, or the owner.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** one list of everyone's work. Use **Everyone**, **Mine** or **Emplio** to narrow it, and **Needs you** at the top for what's waiting on you.

<Tip>People can reply to a reminder with "done" and Emplio updates the task for them.</Tip>
