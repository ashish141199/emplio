Goal: one up-to-date list of leads that Emplio fills in as it works and you can open any time.

**You need**

- To be the owner or an admin to open the **Database** tab. Staff can still add and look up leads through the chat.

<Frame caption="Memory, Database">
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/app/database-light.png" alt="The Database tab showing a Leads table with search and filters" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/app/database-dark.png" alt="The Database tab showing a Leads table with search and filters" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make the table">
    ```text
    Make a Leads table: name, company, phone, city, source, status and next follow-up date.
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add leads from the chat">
    ```text
    Add a lead: Priya Nair, Nair Caterers, Kochi, from IndiaMART, wants 50 kg rice a week.
    ```
    Have an old spreadsheet? Attach it and say "Add everyone in this sheet to Leads."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask about them">
    ```text
    Which leads haven't heard from us in a week?
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the table">
    Click **Memory** in the sidebar, then **Database**, then **Leads**. Search, filter and sort the columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to Excel">
    Click **Export to Excel**. You get what's on screen, with your search and filters applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** a real table Emplio reads and updates as it works, alongside orders, stock or anything else you keep.

<Tip>Turn on **Private** for a table staff shouldn't use, like salaries. Then only the owner and admins can use it.</Tip>
