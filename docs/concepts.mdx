Emplio is one colleague your whole team can talk to. These are the few ideas you need to use it well.

<img src="/images/how-it-works-dark.png" alt="Your team talks to Emplio on the web or Telegram; Emplio works on its own computer, its own browser and your apps, and keeps memory, records and the work list" />

| Idea | What it means |
|---|---|
| [Chat](/guide/chat) | You ask, Emplio does the work. **Chat** at the top of the sidebar is your main chat, the same one as on your phone. |
| [Work](/guide/work) | Tasks for people and for Emplio. It does its own, reminds people of theirs, and shows what needs you. |
| [Memory](/guide/memory) | Plain notes Emplio keeps: **Company** for everyone, **Restricted** for owners and admins, **Mine** for you alone. Every change is kept with full history. |
| [Agents](/guide/agents) | Helpers Emplio sets up for work that keeps coming back, like an Invoice Chaser. Each keeps lessons from its past jobs. |
| [Skills](/guide/skills) | Saved how-tos. Show Emplio a task once in the browser and it turns it into a skill. |
| [Automations](/guide/automations) | Work Emplio does on its own, on a schedule or when something happens in an app. |
| [Approvals](/guide/chat#approve-what-it-sends-or-changes) | Before it sends, changes or deletes anything in your apps, Emplio asks: **Allow**, **Always allow** or **Deny**. Lookups never ask. |
| Its own computer | Each person gets their own computer and browser for Emplio to work in, kept apart from everyone else's. |

<Tip>To stop the approval questions for the whole business, an owner or admin can set **Before sending or changing things** to **Just do it** in [Settings](/guide/settings). Nobody checks the actions then.</Tip>
