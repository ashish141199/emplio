After you sign in, four short steps help Emplio get to know your business. Every step can be skipped, and you can leave halfway. Then your first chat starts.

## The welcome steps

Press **Continue** to move on, **Back** to go back, or **Skip to chat** at the top to jump straight in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tell Emplio about your business">
    Fill in **Business name**, **What do you do?**, **Website** and **Country**. A line or two is plenty. You can also add a price list, brochure or catalogue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the apps you use">
    Press **Connect** next to Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Zoho Books and others, or **See all apps**. See [Integrations](/guide/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Talk to Emplio from your phone">
    Enter **Your mobile number** with the country code, then connect Telegram. See [Phone and Telegram](/guide/channels).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Used another AI before?">
    Bring what ChatGPT, Claude or another AI knows about you. See [Bring your memory](/guide/import). Then press **Meet Emplio**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The setup chat

Your first chat starts as setup. Emplio introduces itself, reads your website and files, and asks only what it still needs: what you sell and to whom, who does what, what it should own, and what needs your OK first. It writes this down in company memory.

<Tip>To brief Emplio again later, open **Settings**, then **Agent**, and press **Start** or **Run again** under **Brief Emplio**.</Tip>

## Good first things to ask

- "Go through this week's enquiries in Gmail and draft replies."
- "Remind Raj to send the quote by Friday."
- "Every weekday at 9, send me yesterday's sales."
- "Give new cafés 30 days' credit from now on."
