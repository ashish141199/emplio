You can talk to Emplio in the web app or on Telegram. Your main **Chat** is the same in both, and Emplio can message you there on its own, like when a report is ready.

## Connect Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    Press your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Channels**. Or press **Connect Telegram** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your mobile number">
    Type it with the country code in **Your mobile number**. This is how Emplio knows it's you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Connect">
    On a computer, scan the code with your phone, or press **Open Telegram**. Then press **Start** in Telegram.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also open the bot and share your number with it. Once connected, the window says **Connected**.

## On Telegram

- Type, or send voice notes, photos and files.
- Tap **Allow** or **Deny** on approvals.
- Send `/new` to start fresh. Emplio keeps notes on what you discussed.
- Send `/switch` to talk to another business you're on.

<Tip>To stop, press **Disconnect** in **Channels**. Emplio won't message you on Telegram after that.</Tip>
