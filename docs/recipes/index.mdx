## Ideas by business type

| Business | Try asking |
|---|---|
| Wholesale or distribution | "Go through today's IndiaMART enquiries, add them to Leads, and draft a reply with our price list." |
| Agency | "Every Friday at 4pm, send me everyone's open tasks and what's late." |
| Shop | "Make a Stock table from this spreadsheet and tell me what's below 10 units." |
| Clinic front desk | "Every evening, list tomorrow's appointments from the calendar and flag any double bookings." |
| Any business | "Every weekday at 9, send me yesterday's sales." |

<Tip>Not sure how to ask? Write it the way you'd brief a new member of staff. Emplio asks if it needs more.</Tip>
