You can use Emplio on Emplio Cloud, with nothing to install, or run it yourself for free. Pick one, sign in, and you are talking to Emplio a few minutes later.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Emplio Cloud" icon="cloud">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open emplio.app">
        Go to [emplio.app](https://emplio.app) and click to get started.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in with your email">
        Enter your **Email**, then the 6-digit **Verification code** we send you, then your **Full name**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Tell Emplio about your business">
        Answer a few short questions. Every step can be skipped. See [Your first 10 minutes](/first-steps).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Pick a plan">
        Choose a plan or a 7-day pass. See [Plans and usage](/cloud/plans).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Run it yourself" icon="server">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Install Docker">
        Get [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/get-docker/). On a Linux server the installer can add it for you.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Run the installer">
        ```bash
        curl -fsSL https://emplio.app/install.sh | bash
        ```
        It asks for an [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/keys) key, your email, and your web address if you have one.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open your sign-in link">
        The installer prints a link that signs you in as the owner. On a computer, Emplio opens at `http://localhost:3000`.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
    <Note>Full details, including servers and HTTPS, are in [Install Emplio](/self-host/install).</Note>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Give it its first job

Type what you need in the chat, the way you'd ask a colleague:

> Go through this week's enquiries in Gmail, add them to our leads, and draft replies.

Emplio shows each step as it works, and asks you before it sends, changes or deletes anything in your apps.
