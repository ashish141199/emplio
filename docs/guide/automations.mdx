Automations are work Emplio does without being asked: on a schedule, when something happens in an app, or when you run one. Each run gets its own chat.

## Make one

The easy way is to ask in a chat:

> Every weekday at 9, send me yesterday's sales.

> When an email comes in from a new customer, add them to our leads.

Emplio sets it up for you.

## Manage them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automations">
    Press your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Automations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check each one">
    You see what starts it, who it runs as, its last run and its next run. Press the last run to open its chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it">
    Use the menu beside it: **Run now**, **Turn off** or **Turn on**, **Edit** or **Delete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If an automation sends or changes things in an app, each of those actions shows a switch. Its runs can't do the ones marked **needs your OK** until you turn their switch on.

Owners and admins can add and change automations. Repeating ones also show on [Work](/guide/work) under **Repeats**.

## Built-in check-ins

Emplio also works on its own through the day, without any automation:

| | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Morning brief** | Today's meetings, what's late, what's due and what needs you. |
| **Background rounds** | Every few hours it looks around, handles what it can, and tells you only what needs you. |
| **End-of-day report** | What got done, what didn't and what's next. |

Each team member gets their own brief and report too. Change the times in [Settings](/guide/settings#schedule).
