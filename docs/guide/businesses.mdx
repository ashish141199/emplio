You can run more than one business from one Emplio account. Each has its own team, memory, work and chats.

## Add a business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Press your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press New business">
    Type the **Business name** and press **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the welcome steps">
    The new business starts with the same short welcome steps. See [Your first 10 minutes](/first-steps).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch business

Press your name, then pick a business under **Business**. A tick shows the one that's open. Everything on screen then belongs to that business.

On Telegram, send `/switch` and pick the business.

<Note>Someone who already belongs to another business can't be added to your team yet. Each business you create yourself shows in your list.</Note>
