Used another AI before? Bring what it learned about you and your work, so Emplio doesn't start from zero. Business facts go into company memory, the rest into your own notes.

## The quick way, for any AI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bring your memory">
    It's the last welcome step. Later, open **Settings**, then **Agent**, and press **Start** under **Bring your memory**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the AI">
    **Claude**, **ChatGPT**, **Gemini**, **Grok**, **OpenClaw**, **Hermes** or **Another AI**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the prompt">
    Press **Copy the prompt**, send it to that AI in a new chat, and copy its whole answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste and save">
    Paste the answer, take out anything you don't want Emplio to have, like passwords, and press **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bring old chats too

For Claude, ChatGPT, OpenClaw and Hermes, press the **Bring its export instead** link and follow the steps shown:

| AI | What to pick |
|---|---|
| Claude | The files from Claude's export email |
| ChatGPT | The zip from **Settings**, **Data controls**, **Export data** on chatgpt.com |
| OpenClaw | Your `.openclaw` folder, after stopping OpenClaw |
| Hermes | Your `.hermes` folder, after quitting Hermes |

Your old chats stay private to you. Emplio sorts the rest and tells you in your main chat when it's done.

<Note>Passwords, keys and program files in those folders are never sent.</Note>
