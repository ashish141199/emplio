Emplio works alongside your team like a new member of staff. Ask it for something the way you'd ask a person, on the web or on Telegram, and it does the work, not just the writing.

<img src="/images/demo.gif" alt="Emplio answering wholesale enquiries, making a quote, and learning a new credit rule" />

## What it can do

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Does real work" icon="monitor">
    Its own computer and browser. It opens websites, fills in forms and makes quotes, invoices and spreadsheets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Works in your apps" icon="plug">
    Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Zoho Books and more.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Runs the to-do list" icon="list-checks">
    Takes tasks, chases your team for theirs, and tells you what needs you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Learns as it goes" icon="brain">
    Tell it a rule once, or show it a task once, and it remembers.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Two ways to use it

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Emplio Cloud" icon="cloud" href="/quickstart">
    Sign up at emplio.app. Nothing to install.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run it yourself" icon="server" href="/self-host/index">
    Free and open source. Install it on your computer or server.
  </Card>
</Columns>
