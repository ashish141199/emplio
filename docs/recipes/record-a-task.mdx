Goal: a job you do on a website, like a supplier portal or a government form, becomes something Emplio can do for you.

**You need**

- A chat where Emplio has opened its browser.
- A microphone, if you want to talk it through.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the site in Emplio's browser">
    ```text
    Open our supplier portal in your browser.
    ```
    The browser opens beside the chat. If you closed it, click **Live browser** to bring it back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start recording">
    Click **Show Emplio how**. Pick **Record voice** to talk as you go, or **Clicks only**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do the task once">
    Click and type as you normally would. Say why you do each step: "I pick the oldest order first." You can mute any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Done**. Or **Cancel** to throw the recording away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    Emplio makes a skill from it and tells you in a line what it can now do. Next time just ask:

    ```text
    Place this week's restock order on the supplier portal.
    ```
    Or type **/** in the chat box and pick the skill.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**What you get:** a skill, for the whole business or just for you, depending on who would use it.

<Warning>What you save while recording really saves. Finish within 30 minutes: a recording left open longer stops and is not kept.</Warning>

<Tip>Passwords aren't recorded. If the site needs a login, click **Take control** to sign in yourself, or save it under **Passwords & keys**.</Tip>
