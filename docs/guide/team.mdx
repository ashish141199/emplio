Everyone on your team can chat with Emplio. The **Team** page lists your people and Emplio's [agents](/guide/agents). Owners and admins can add and manage people.

## Add someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Add person">
    At the top of the **Team** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    Type their **Email** and **Full name**. Tick **Admin** if they should see restricted files and manage the team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Add and email">
    They get an email to join and can chat with Emplio right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Someone who already belongs to another business can't be added yet.

## Manage someone

Press the menu beside a person to:

- **Make admin** or **Make staff**
- **Set who they report to**, or change it. A manager sees and updates their people's tasks.
- **Remove** them. They lose access to Emplio and this business. You can invite them again later.

## Roles

| | Owner | Admin | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chat with Emplio | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Restricted notes and the **Database** tab | Yes | Yes | No |
| Everyone's tasks and files | Yes | Yes | Only their own |
| Manage the team, settings and automations | Yes | Yes | No |

Each person gets their own computer and browser for Emplio to work in, kept apart from everyone else's. Their connected apps and saved passwords are theirs too.

<Tip>You can also ask in a chat: "Add priya@example.com as staff. She reports to Raj."</Tip>
